Privacy Policy for Voting Poster Maker

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Voting Poster Maker is an application designed to help users create and edit voting posters.

Data Collection and Use

The app is designed to work offline. The developer does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information through the app.

Photos and Files

Photos selected by users are used for poster creation and editing. Users should save or export their work using the features provided by the app.

Advertising

The current version does not intentionally include advertising services.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play listing.
